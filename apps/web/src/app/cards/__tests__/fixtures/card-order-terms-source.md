# Living Business Card: Order Terms

Draft dated 25 October 2026.

## 1. Who we are

1. Your card is supplied by Unite-Group Nexus Pty Ltd (ABN 95 691 477 844) ("we", "us").
2. Payment is processed by our related company, CARSI Pty Ltd. That name appears on the checkout page and on your bank statement. Unite-Group Nexus Pty Ltd is responsible for your order and any refund.
3. When you pay, you agree to these terms. Please read them before you pay.

## 2. What you get

Your card is digital: a short film and your own web page. We do not print or post a physical card.

**Ready-made, AUD 249 (GST included)**
1. You pick one of our ten card films. It ends on your logo and details.
2. Your own card web page with that film, your business name and your contact details.
3. You approve a private preview before it goes live.

**Made for you, AUD 449 (GST included)**
1. We make a film for your business, instead of you picking one of the ten.
2. You approve a private preview before it goes live.
3. Your own card web page with that film, your business name and your contact details.

We supply only what this section lists. Anything else is quoted separately before we start.

## 3. Ordering and payment

1. You order through the payment link for the card you want.
2. Prices are in Australian dollars and include GST. You pay the full price up front.
3. We currently accept orders only from customers in Australia.
4. Your order starts when your payment clears. Unite-Group Nexus Pty Ltd emails you a tax invoice when you pay.

## 4. What you give us

1. After you pay, we email you from contact@unite-group.in and ask for your logo, business name, the contact details you want shown, and (if you like) where we met.
2. You promise that you own, or have permission to use, everything you send us, and that it is accurate and lawful.
3. If someone makes a claim against us because something you sent us breaks their rights or the law, you will pay our reasonable costs of that claim. You won't have to pay for any part we caused.
4. We may refuse or remove material that we reasonably believe is unlawful, misleading, offensive, or breaks someone else's rights.

## 5. Timeline

1. We aim to send your private preview within 5 business days after we have everything in section 4.
2. We aim to make your card live within 1 business day after you approve it.
3. These are targets, not promises. If we will be late, we will email you.
4. If you don't send us what we need under section 4, we will email you two reminders, 14 days apart. If you still don't reply within 60 days after the second reminder, we may close your order. We will refund two-thirds (2/3) of the price you paid. This is the same for every card type. This does not limit your rights under the Australian Consumer Law: if your card is faulty, wrong or not as described, you are entitled to a full repair, replacement or refund. If we have already sent you a preview, section 6.5 applies instead.

## 6. Revisions

1. Before your card goes live you can ask for changes to the preview. Each set of changes you send counts as one round.
2. Ready-made includes 3 rounds. Made for you includes 10 rounds.
3. A round covers corrections and small changes. A new concept or full redesign is not a revision; we quote it first.
4. More rounds than included are quoted before we do them.
5. If you do not reply to a preview within 14 days, we email a reminder. If you still do not reply within 7 days after that reminder, the preview counts as approved and we make the card live. The reminder email will say clearly that your card will go live if you do not reply. Going live this way does not stop you asking us to fix mistakes we made, and it does not affect your rights under the Australian Consumer Law.

## 7. Hosting

1. We host your card for 12 months from the day it goes live. There is no monthly fee.
2. We email you at least 30 days before hosting ends with the renewal price. Hosting does not renew unless you choose to pay.
3. If you do not renew, the card goes offline when hosting ends. You can ask for a copy of your finished film before then.
4. We take reasonable care to keep your card online, but it may be offline for short periods, for example during maintenance.
5. We may suspend a card that is being used for anything unlawful or misleading. We will email you first unless the law or urgency stops us.
6. If we ever have to stop hosting for our own reasons, we will give you at least 30 days' email notice, a copy of your finished film, and a fair refund for the hosting time you have not had.

## 8. Changes after your card is live

1. One small change, such as a new phone number, email address or logo file, within 30 days of going live is free.
2. Other changes are quoted before we do them.

## 9. Refunds and cancellations

1. Cancel by email before we send your first preview and we refund you in full.
2. After we send your first preview, we do not give refunds for change of mind.
3. If something is wrong with your card, email contact@unite-group.in. If it is a small problem, we will fix it within a reasonable time. If we don't, you can cancel and get a refund, or have someone else fix it and claim the reasonable cost from us. If it is a major problem, you can choose to cancel and get a refund, or keep the card and get money back for the drop in its value. You may also claim for other loss you could reasonably expect from the problem.
4. We pay refunds back to the payment card or account you used, within 10 business days after you are entitled to them.
5. Nothing in this section limits your rights under the Australian Consumer Law (section 12).

## 10. Who owns what

1. You keep all rights in your logo, business name and trademarks. You let us use them to make, host and show your card.
2. Our films, designs, templates and tools stay ours. Some music and footage is licensed to us; you may not take it out of the card and use it separately.
3. You may keep and share your finished card and film as a whole, for example on social media, including a copy you get under section 7. Licensed music and footage may only be used as part of that film.
4. We may show your card as an example of our work. If you would rather we did not, email us and we will stop.

## 11. Limits on our responsibility

1. We do not promise that your card will win you work or customers.
2. Our cards are made for business use. They are not the kind of thing people usually buy for personal or household use. So, where the law allows and it is fair and reasonable, if we break a consumer guarantee we will either do the work again, or pay for someone else to do it again; or, for a film file we gave you, replace or repair it, or pay for that. This does not take away your right to a refund or to cancel if there is a major failure.
3. Apart from your rights under the Australian Consumer Law (section 12), we are not responsible for indirect loss such as lost profits, and the most we will pay for an order is the price you paid for it.
4. These limits do not apply where the law says they cannot, including to loss caused by our fraud or reckless conduct.

## 12. Your rights under the Australian Consumer Law

1. Our services come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law.
2. Nothing in these terms excludes, restricts or changes those rights or any other right that cannot lawfully be excluded. If anything here conflicts with that law, the law wins.

## 13. Privacy

1. We use what you send us to make and host your card, reply to you and manage your order.
2. People who view your card can send you an enquiry or feedback. Unite-Group Nexus Pty Ltd stores it and emails it to you. Our staff who look after the database can see it.
3. We use cloud, email and payment providers to run your card. Your information is stored mainly in Australia (our database is in Sydney), but some of these providers may also handle it in other countries, such as the United States. We take reasonable steps to make sure these providers protect it the way Australian privacy law requires.
4. Our related company CARSI Pty Ltd handles your payment and sees your payment details. We do not sell your personal information.
5. You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing contact@unite-group.in. We will delete it unless the law says we must keep it. Our privacy policy explains more, including how to complain and how we handle complaints.
6. We keep order and enquiry records while your card is hosted and for 12 months after, then delete them, except records we must keep by law (such as tax records).

1. We may update these terms for future orders. Your order stays on the terms shown when you paid.
2. These terms are governed by the laws of Queensland, Australia.
3. Contact: contact@unite-group.in. We aim to reply within 2 business days.
4. If you have a complaint, email us. If we can't fix it, you can contact your state or territory fair trading office (in Queensland, the Office of Fair Trading).
